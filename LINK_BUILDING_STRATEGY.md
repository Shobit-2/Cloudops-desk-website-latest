## 5. Competitor Backlink Analysis

- **Ahrefs** — Analyze competitor backlink profiles
- **SEMrush** — Find competitor referring domains
- **Moz Link Explorer** — Discover link opportunities

### Process
1. Identify top 5 competitors in "IT job support India"
2. Export their backlink profiles
3. Find domains linking to competitors but not you
4. Reach out to those domains with better content
5. Monitor new competitor links monthly
